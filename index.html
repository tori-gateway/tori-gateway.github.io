Mass surveillance

Mass surveillance and the unchecked collection of personal data have become common practices in the online world. Governments and corporations constantly track our digital footprints, threatening our right to privacy. In contrast, the darknet offers a refuge for those who wish to protect their identity and communication from ubiquitous surveillance.

Privacy isn't a crime, it's a right.

This is a belief that reminds us everyone has the right to anonymity and security when navigating the internet. On TORi search engine, there are no compromises between privacy and freedom. We don't leave traces, and we don't track what you search for. Your data remains yours.

What is TORi, and what does it do?

TORi is a secure, privacy-focused indexer designed specifically for the Tor network. Unlike traditional search engines/indexers, TORi operates in a way that respects your anonymity and keeps your browsing habits private. By using the Tor network's encryption and routing, it ensures that no one can trace your online activity back to you.

TORi allows users to search for darkweb websites safely and anonymously without leaving digital footprints. It doesn't track your searches, collect personal data, or engage in any form of surveillance. Whether you're looking for information, resources, or just browsing the darknet, TORi protects your privacy every step of the way.

How do I access it?

To visit TORi, you need to have access to Tor network. You can achieve this by having tor proxy, or downloading and using the tor browser. To make sure you are connected to Tor, you can check on check.torproject.org.
After you are set up, click on the "Visit TORi (requires )" button, and you should be automatically redirected to TORi's .onion website.
